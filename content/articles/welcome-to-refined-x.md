Refined-X is a personal publish starter. Humans get an editorial reading experience; agents get structured discovery endpoints generated from the same content root.

## What you configure

- `contentRoot` — where your public Markdown/YAML live
- `publicDir` / `outDir` — static assets and build output
- optional Public Ask worker URLs for NLWeb `/ask`
