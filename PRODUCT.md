# Product

## Register

brand

Brand is the default register for Refined-X. Interactive surfaces (`/ask`, header Ask overlay) follow **product** patterns: explicit affordances, immediate feedback, keyboard-first flows.

## Users

- **Primary**: Engineers and makers publishing long-form writing, projects, and curated FAQ.
- **Secondary**: Collaborators evaluating credibility via about/projects.
- **Infrastructure**: External agents consuming `llms.txt`, OpenAPI, `/answers/`, and JSON APIs.

## Product Purpose

Refined-X is an **agent-ready personal publish starter**: opinionated public content schema, editorial design, and machine-readable discovery generated from the same corpus. Optional NLWeb Public Ask is a sibling deploy, not required for the static site.

## Brand Personality

**前瞻 · 利落 · 实验** (tech-forward, crisp, experimental) — confident peer voice, monochrome editorial restraint with deliberate energy on conversational entry points.

## Design Principles

1. **编辑优先，工具次之**: Brand surfaces lead with typography; product surfaces prioritize input clarity.
2. **事实可引用**: Public structure must be agent-citable.
3. **单色为骨，动效为脉**: Neutrals carry identity; motion stays bounded to AI affordances.
4. **同行对话，而非宣讲**.
5. **渐进智能**: Static Ask must look credible today; leave room for NLWeb without a full rebrand.
